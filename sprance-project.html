Sprance | A social fitness and run-tracking app.

Category Social fitness, Run-tracking
Year 2026
My role Product designer

Overview

Sprance is a social fitness and run-tracking mobile application designed to turn running into an enjoyable, connected habit while capturing granular race telemetry, facilitating local runner discovery, and driving motivation through gamified leaderboards.

Design Process

For the design process, I followed 5 critical steps. Note that the steps are non linear

[Empathize] → [Define Problem & Goals] → [Ideate] → [Prototype] → [Test & Iterate]

The Problem

Running is one of the most accessible forms of exercise, but users find it hard to enjoy & track important data from each race taken.

The Goal

To design an intuitive, high-visibility mobile app that makes running inherently enjoyable while effortlessly capturing critical race data.

Research

The research phase is to understand the user's feelings, behaviors, & pain points and also validate my hypothesis without bringing my own biases into it. It includes, user interviews and competitive analysis.

Competitor's Analysis

To understand how existing platforms attempt to solve the research gap, we analyzed three companies that have tried to solve this & related problems.

They include:

Strava
MapMyRun
Go App

Based on data from our analysis. There is a distinct gap in the market for an app that balances heavy data visualization with high-enjoyment social features.

User Personas

To ensure the Sprance app addresses the full spectrum of user friction, we synthesized our research into two distinct archetypes: the hesitant newcomer and the efficiency-driven veteran.

Defining The Core Features

To solve the gap between raw data tracking and community enjoyment, Sprance was built around four core functional pillars.

  1. Frictionless Mid-Run Tracking- The Glanceable UI
  2. Comprehensive Race Telemetry Hub
  3. Contextual Post-Race Summaries
  4. Hyper-Local Social Discovery & Leaderboards

User Impacts

  1. Smarter, Context-Driven Training
  2. Safer, Distraction-Free Runs
  3. Sustained Motivation & Reduced Churn

Anticipated Business Impact

A product that reduces friction directly improves business performance metrics.

  1. Retention Through Habit-Looping: The combination of personal data tracking (investment) and community leaderboards (variable reward) creates a highly “sticky” product, directly increasing Daily Active Users (DAU) and reducing app uninstalls compared to basic, isolated step-trackers.
