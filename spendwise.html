SpendWise | Humanizing Personal Wealth & Credit Health Through Character Design

Category Finance
Year 2026
My role Illustrator, Animator

Overview

A personal finance, budgeting, and credit health platform that uses character-driven storytelling to demystify complex money management.

Project Background & Challenge

Managing personal finances, monitoring credit scores, and tackling debt frequently trigger user anxiety, leading to low long-term engagement with standard fintech apps. Spreadsheets, sterile data graphs, and cold banking interfaces intimidate non-expert users rather than motivate them.

The objective was to design a brand mascot and emotional visual system capable of breaking down the stigma around personal money management. By leveraging an elephant—the universal symbol of long-term memory, wisdom, and steadfast stability—the visual language transforms stressful financial audits and mundane budgeting tasks into an encouraging, gamified journey

Mascot Metaphor & Visual Strategy

  • The “Elephant Never Forgets” Principle: Grounding the brand around an elephant naturally builds confidence in automated recurring payments, memory-based expense tracking, and credit reporting.
  • Approachable Geometry: Soft curves, rounded ears, and friendly dot eyes soften technical banking interactions.
  • Grounded Color Palette: A calm, stable forest green hue serves as the primary anchor, conveying growth, prosperity, and safety without the aggressive visual alerts typical of traditional financial apps.
